# Continuing story life — 0.16.0

## What changed

The previous throughline could become an exclusive incident agenda. Its review checkpoint also let a new wider-planning responsibility inherit an old scene planner's narrow view. More instructions about "long term" did not reliably fix either constraint.

- The wider workshop now records private `storyLife`: scope, full premise, current episode, continuing life, and links to prepared futures. Initial orientation protects the opening from optional-context removal and revisits accepted choices; established orientation uses ordinary incremental review.
- The throughline no longer monopolizes selection. Up to two complementary futures can reach the writer from the existing three-trajectory shelf. Bounded stories can still select one or none, without manufacturing a sequel.
- Current action remains in a private ledger. The active scene contract returns empty `selected_material`; the host composes writer material from selected future openings and their intermediate/farther possibilities. Openings still carry genuine encounter conditions, not ungrounded access.
- The stage handoff explicitly lists revised, retired and retained trajectory IDs. Retired wider ideas cannot reappear through stale local links. NPC work can continue locally without retaining an obsolete wider trajectory.
- The combined writer packet must fit its existing 1,000-token envelope. A malformed or oversized composition consumes the existing shared repair allowance, not an extra loop. Normal preparation still uses two requests, with a three-request ceiling.
- Old saved plans remain readable. The new orientation is established on their next successful preparation; loading this release does not rewrite accepted history or saved prose.

## Verification

The full `npm test` suite passes: 1,173 tests, zero failures. Deterministic coverage exercises migration/opening preservation, ordinary incremental review, two-future composition, privacy, renewed access, retired links, packet bounds, repair, saved-state compatibility, source invalidation and browser lifecycle behavior. Handwritten fixtures establish contract behavior, not creative quality.

`scripts/evaluate-story-life.mjs` runs the production two-stage path against an external freeze, without generating a writer reply or saving live preparation. Artifacts stay outside the repository. It checks frozen-state immutability and reports whether live source files changed concurrently.
