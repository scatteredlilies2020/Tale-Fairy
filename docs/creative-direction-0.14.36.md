# Creative direction first — 0.14.36

The desired contribution is an interesting direction for the RP, not increasingly
concrete activities or scene details. The previous usefulness check put too much
weight on specificity. Broad guidance is useful when it proposes a distinctive
possibility; naming activities or promising generic improvement is not enough.

The active single-pass contract and writer-material schema now prioritize what
could change in a relationship, shared purpose, use of competence or world process,
and what that could open up. Details are optional and only distinguish the direction.
The writer and player retain the manifestation, route, choices and outcome. This
does not require escalation, reconciliation, a fixed arc or constant novelty.

No storage migration, additional model pass, automatic rewrite of saved plans or
chat/settings edits are introduced. The four-reply freshness bound and failed-pass
recovery remain unchanged.
