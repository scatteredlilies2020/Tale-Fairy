# Planner attention and writer material — 0.14.21

## Engineering results, not a live-quality claim

The original selector could choose twelve current-investigation records and zero unrelated records from a 24-record notebook. The new regression uses that same distribution, both with and without family labels: six current records and six wider records enter the working view. A stable 24-pass sequence verifies that every record receives attention even when all family labels are identical. Selection and repeated token-budget fitting do not mutate storage; successful planner merges advance a review cursor, never story time or proposal status.

At small budgets, a complete local and wider record remain represented where available. Native-schema and prompt-only envelopes are tested with a simulated provider counter 20% above local estimates. The audit reports the records actually present after fitting. If protected instructions and witnesses cannot fit, the existing visible budget failure is preferable to silently deleting them. This is a minimum coverage guarantee, not exhaustive recall or a guarantee that every selected record is semantically independent.

Writer tests pass distinct public material and private notebook sentinels through parsing, state persistence, chat/text injection and actual generation assembly. Public material reaches the request; notebook middle/future and dependency prose do not. Existing source-prefix, in-flight, regeneration and archive protections remain covered. Families and dependency prose also survive persistence and constrain consolidation.

The unchanged baseline passed on Node 24. The system Node 18.16 test environment lacked global Web Crypto, causing six existing archive-test failures; that was not evidence of six production narrative regressions. Use a modern Node runtime for `npm test`.

## Required live comparison before claiming a narrative improvement

Run the current baseline, this candidate and no Tale Fairy with the same saved preset, writer/planner models and initial source. Keep reports outside the repository and never save experimental responses into the original chat. Record full candidate identity, source hash, actual writer packet, notebook before/after, token cost and latency. Use multiple trials per case and sequences longer than a full-review interval, not just a single continuation.

| Case | What to examine |
| --- | --- |
| RPG party, long village investigation | Distinct witnesses are not counted as independent arcs. Wider interests remain available without forced interruption. Finish or leave the mystery; the next region is not automatically another branch of it. |
| Slice of life, extended conversation | The conversation can remain worthwhile while other relationships, activities and interests stay prepared. No compulsory surprise, conflict, hesitation or generic warnings. |
| Country sim, one budget debate | Other institutional, civic or economic possibilities retain their own causes instead of every record serving the debate. No invented player policy decisions. |
| Life sim, work and family | Concrete initiatives can develop through ordinary life. Refusal or redirection is respected without replacement hooks repeating the same offer. |
| Closed scenario | No quota-driven unrelated subplot. Wider review can simply retain different aspects of the supplied scenario. |
| Several replies before the planner finishes | Writer material remains useful without prescribing the obsolete next action. No waiting on the planner or cancellation storm. |

Score causal independence by whether a development still has a reason to exist if the current problem disappears, not by record count, category names or model self-labels. Separately score concrete usefulness, repeated instructions, preset/style interference, continuity, knowledge and player ownership. Inspect both private preparation and actual prose; retaining a record is not proof of using it effectively. Report failed trials and model variance.
