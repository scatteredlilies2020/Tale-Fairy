# Campaign-scope correction — 2026-09-18

Status: partial improvement, not satisfactory campaign-scope acceptance.

This records the earlier scope-only attempt, not the final 0.14.24 verdict.
The subsequent [independent-development revision](independent-development-0.14.24.md)
uses a different planning premise and has its own uptake and review evidence.

## Change

The planner is asked to choose wider undertakings before actors and events,
within the RP's own scale. Private `development` precedes `initiative` and
`plot_points` in the response schema. Events should reach beyond the current
episode or introduce a wider-world interruption, rather than supply the next
step of the existing dispute. Broader does not mean more dangerous.

The `campaign-wide-v1` scope marker triggers a one-time replacement of older
event plans. Their text and topical IDs are excluded from the planning request;
all earlier player contributions are eligible as protected source. A successful
pass archives complete prior subjects and installs the new repertoire atomically.
This archive operation is not a claim of fictional completion. A failed pass
leaves the original state intact. Legacy essay conversion retains its existing
objective/rewrite checks; later campaign-wide reviews retain normal IDs and
unplayed events. No extra AI call, button, writer instruction or live-chat edit.

## Verification and remaining work

686 offline tests pass. Added checks cover schema ordering, event-only injection,
full-source reframe, fresh subject IDs, complete archive retention, atomic failure,
bounded empty results and return to normal review after the one-time upgrade.

The mechanism now permits higher-tier planning; model outputs do not reliably
achieve it. Remaining work is to make wider undertakings—not local actor agendas—
the unit of planning, and ensure actual injected events carry that scope with
less repetitive obstruction. No fixed plot ending or scene-by-scene script is
desired. Do not infer satisfaction from this version number or test count.
