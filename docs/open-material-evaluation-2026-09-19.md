# Open material evaluation — 2026-09-19 (research history)

This record preserves earlier candidates that were **not release-ready** and
were not deployed. The baseline during those experiments was 0.14.33. No live
chat or saved setting was edited by the evaluations.

The later private-background/discovery experiment, failed transition gate and

## Acceptance criteria

- Inspect the actual serialized writer packet, not just private plans or JSON validity.
- Select distinct opportunities, interests or processes with meaningful reach
  beyond the immediate task; preserve private mid-/long-term aims and witnesses.
- No assigned player decisions, exhaustive outcome trees, writing-style advice,
  routine-task replenishment, obligatory interruption or genre quota.
- Available does not mean already accepted or enacted. Do not withhold every
  useful wider opportunity while waiting for explicit player initiation.
- Keep the normal single-call review and automatic maintenance. No critic loop,
  manual selection decision, silent semantic regex deletion or extra runtime call.

## Implemented candidate

- Removed the instruction requiring conditional branches and the blanket demand
  to spell every future dependency with `If`. Necessary prerequisites still matter.
- Clarified open conditions, distinct whole-packet contributions, useful longer-term
  effects and available-but-not-enacted opportunities. Added positive representation

  excluded as a copying template, while episode identifiers, durable preparation

- Arrival (004): the music entry recognized Mere, but a kept travel entry still

- Private development and later reviews (005–007): Jo and Sef received separate

  musical activity; the later invitation to consider wider possibilities did not
  restore the wider horizon. This reproduces the overlap beyond the real RP.

**The trajectory fails the satisfaction criteria despite 7/7 valid responses.**
Independent private NPC aims need not produce independent writer entries. Test
whole-packet selection that can represent several subjects together, and fresh
applicability assessment rather than relying on a bare keep decision. Do not
replace these with a genre quota, forced novelty or a second model call.

A further external multi-subject-schema diagnostic on the exact final-stage input
produced one shared seasonal-planning circumstance referencing all four aims,
rather than two copies of the duet. It restored broader travel/artistic interests
and current Mere applicability. This is promising structural evidence, not a
validated runtime result: it mostly elaborates the present planning conversation,
and has not been tested through the full trajectory or adapted to storage/evidence
maintenance. Do not ship or claim completion based on that single raw sample.

## Remaining work

### Whole-story selection implementation and checks

The main runtime now uses `story-selection.js`: one response contains a complete
`selected_material` snapshot independently of private subject updates and exact
witnessed progress. A selection references one or several retained/new subject
ids. Shared circumstances can serve several aims without duplicate writer entries.
There is no per-subject selection quota and no bare `keep` operation. Empty
selection withdraws the packet, not the aims. Failed validation remains atomic.

`selected-material.js` validates identities/references and serializes only source
owners plus open material. It does not claim semantic validation. The adapter
reuses the established evidence transaction with explicit empty legacy selections;
it does not repair an AI response, call a second model, or delete the evidence ledger.
Changed selections and legacy selections are archived. Actual system/schema token
costs participate in evidence fitting before the host chooses a context window.

795 tests pass, including nine new contract/migration tests and two new host
tests for multi-subject serialization/cache/retries and atomic failure without
repair. The host harness now imports the real new runtime contract; old protocol
unit tests remain as compatibility coverage rather than posing as integration
coverage for the new path.

### Standalone contract comparison

The inherited, repeatedly amended creative prompt was replaced with one standalone
contract under 1,100 words. The established transactional evidence validator remains
unchanged. A scope-reset regression test additionally verifies that old whole-story
selections are withheld and archived only after successful replacement.
**796/796 deterministic tests pass** for this candidate.

The v4 frozen real review validated on its first call but produced four overlapping
verification/accountability entries. Open future wording alone did not solve the
selection problem. The touring progression initially retained the routine hall
search as a separate opportunity, then dropped it when the delivery was resolved.
Its fourth call was rate-limited (HTTP 429, six prompts/minute); this is a transport
failure, not a rejected creative response, and the progression stopped there.
Artifacts: `%TEMP%/tf-whole-story-v4-*`; bundle
`306b252e8cab4b177705a4ad10b1d930ca63e36f81423881eb0545ab97220d99`.
These results are not release evidence of satisfactory selection.

The dominant failure is **selection**, not packet formatting. Old-plan anchoring
is a plausible contributor, not an established sole cause: fresh preparation and
several attempts to hide old wording also reproduced the dominant source mechanism.
Next investigate the relationship between retained private drafts, the current
episode and subject-by-subject selection. Compare a coherent shorter selection
contract against these saved failures rather than adding more prohibitions or
another model call. Preserve genuinely useful longer-term aims and accepted
progress; do not silently erase them to make an evaluation look better.

Before release: rerun real-plan review and multi-step touring progression with a
stable final candidate; inspect packets for duplicate mechanisms, meaningful wider
reach and agency. Run the full deterministic suite, bump browser cache versions,
and document remaining limitations. Do not mark the goal achieved based only on
unit tests, a self-reported planner assessment or a cherry-picked successful sample.

## Integrated horizon candidate

The separate-entry format repeatedly encouraged miniature plots, even after
prompt changes. The final representation instead selects zero or one integrated
packet with available circumstances, mid-term possibilities and long-term
possibilities. Multiple independent activities can coexist in this packet; it
does not impose one event or storyline. Stable private subjects and the strict
accepted-progress transaction remain separate. The planner receives a standalone
contract, not accumulated legacy instructions. The notebook shows the shared
horizons once and identifies contributing subjects without reporting empty
legacy per-subject fields as an empty selection.

Stable evaluated source-bundle hash:
`7f77cdbf119b91c5488fdb78d036484c30371ffa79eb54fb410eef0d2231a582`.
The test sequence was fixed in advance: three independent reviews of the same
saved 93-message RP and previous plan, four other premises, and a seven-stage
touring progression. Each review uses one ordinary request, without a critic,
response repair or automatic retry. Requests are spaced to respect the provider's
rate limit. All private evaluation artifacts remain outside the repository.
