# Useful preparation and bounded freshness — 0.14.35

## Problem and policy

The reviewed local RP had a prefix-valid plan from an earlier meeting. A later
failed attempt consumed the long review interval, while the writer continued
receiving the same selection through a different scene. Prefix authentication
alone cannot establish the currency of scene-facing material.

`campaignUsable` remains the durable source-validity check. The separate
`campaignMaterialUsable` expires writer material at four accepted assistant
appends, or a shorter configured interval. Historical retry packets are checked
against their own pre-response source. Already-frozen in-flight requests are
not rewritten. Expiration does not delete preparation or call a model.

Successful automatic reviews use the same bounded interval. A failed attempt
becomes eligible after one new accepted assistant reply, not after a user-only
append or unchanged-source reload. Stop retains normal cadence. No timer retry,
critic, repair or additional provider request was added to a pass.

## Creative changes

The single planning contract asks for actual subject matter for possible
activities, not generic improvement, reputation or rapport. Established player
competence is usable capacity rather than an invitation to perpetual testing.
The contract permits specific unrealized possibilities without making offers,
agreements or outcomes canon. Unresolved conflict remains valid but need not
occupy every available activity. Accessible NPCs do not automatically expose
their private history or expertise.

## Limits

The age bound is not semantic scene recognition: suggestions can become
irrelevant sooner than four replies. A manual review remains useful after an
abrupt change. More frequent review costs more than the former 12-reply setting.
The planner can still be bland or mishandle knowledge boundaries, and the writer
may ignore a good suggestion. These changes stop indefinite stale injection;
they do not prove improved long-run RP quality.
