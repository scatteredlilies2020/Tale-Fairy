# Scope and flexibility — 0.14.25

The live 0.14.24 plan contained three useful but block-centred undertakings.
Several later events assumed their earlier proposals succeeded. The latest
accepted RP reply predated that preparation; it could not establish its uptake.

## Changes

- Look beyond the current episode's dominant mechanism. Use source relationships,
  the larger setting and earlier wishes, not just new owners of similar tasks.
- Do not turn a few local decisions into a permanent player mission or make the
  wider world a queue of requests for the player's approval.
- Develop playable middles and later reach before producing concise events.
- Put every unestablished prerequisite inside its own event. A later date is not
  evidence that another proposal happened. Avoid fixed outcomes, incidental
  headcounts and scene choreography.

These are semantic planning instructions, not a keyword validator or a guarantee
that a model obeys them. The implementation keeps the single-call transaction,
event-only writer payload and unchanged UI. Scope `independent-developments-v2`
reframes older preparations on the next normal pass. Full old records survive in
the archive after success; failures preserve the current state. Accepted play,
not the discarded drafts, supplies continuity.

## Final assessment

The subsequent single-call review retained all four subject IDs. It moved the
active search and gate problem into the episode without retiring the family
undertaking, replaced the initial sighting with a possible standing messenger
arrangement, and preserved uncertainty around the names. The copying network
remained available. Other NPC projects advanced into proposed later work; those
proposals are not proof that the later days have already occurred.

This meets the bounded practical target: the planner supplies broader material,
the selected writer uses it after a scene transition, an adopted thread develops
into activity, and review keeps its identity and progress. It does not eliminate
every numerical detail, repetitive mechanism or writer-created permission gate.
Future conditions remain a semantic instruction rather than a deterministic
guarantee. Long-term viability beyond these branches still requires play.
